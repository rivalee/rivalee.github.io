Rebecca Cottrell

Painter living in South East London

I make paintings of strange objects, creatures, structures and staged worlds. I return to these forms because they suggest something unknown, just beyond ordinary reality. I want my work to create a certain feeling: an uncanniness, a recognition of something that's been forgotten.
